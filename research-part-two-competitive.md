## 2 · Per-competitor teardown

### 2.1 Wild Fusion — Lagos senior incumbent
*wildfusions.com · scored from search corpus*

- **Positioning sentence.** "Africa's leading digital-led integrated marketing communications agency." Pan-African — Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya.
- **Hero.** Integrated marketing communications for large brands; emphasis on scale and heritage (decade-plus, first certified Google AdWords partner in Nigeria).
- **Conversion mechanic.** Contact / quote request. Enterprise sales motion.
- **Lead magnet.** Case studies; no interactive diagnostic.
- **Pricing transparency.** Opaque — enterprise retainer.
- **Mobile / build.** Established agency site; adequate, not a performance leader.
- **AI-search readiness.** Silent. No LLM-citation posture, no `llms.txt` signal.
- **Named methodology.** None distinct — positioned on service breadth and client logos (Unilever, telecom, financial services).
- **GSLEY's gap to exploit.** Wild Fusion is broad, brand-advertising-led, and enterprise-heavy. No productisation, no AI-native worldview, no named methodology, no institutional-AI-governance lens. GSLEY out-positions on focus and method, not on scale — and should never compete on heritage.

### 2.2 Oxgital — Lagos AI-positioned challenger
*oxgital.com · scored from search corpus*

- **Positioning sentence.** "The best digital marketing agency in Lagos" with "AI-powered marketing solutions designed to automate processes and improve campaign performance."
- **Hero.** Data-driven, measurable results; frames marketing as "a strategic investment, not a monthly cost" — a genuinely good line.
- **Conversion mechanic.** Contact + an aggressive listicle-SEO play (they publish "top agencies in Nigeria" lists ranking themselves first).
- **Lead magnet.** Blog content.
- **Pricing transparency.** Opaque.
- **Mobile / build.** Adequate.
- **AI-search readiness.** Partial — the only Lagos incumbent claiming AI, but at the level of "AI-powered marketing tools," not LLM-citation strategy.
- **Named methodology.** None distinct.
- **GSLEY's gap to exploit.** Oxgital is the closest local AI claim, which means GSLEY must out-specify it: tool-level AI versus an AI-native operating-system worldview, with an institutional lens Oxgital does not carry. The editorial restraint of "Engineered Precision" is the visible separator.

### 2.3 SA Media — Lagos healthcare specialist
*samediaplus.com · live fetch*

- **Positioning sentence.** "Top Healthcare Digital Marketing Agency in Lagos, Nigeria."
- **Hero.** "We help doctors, dentists, and other healthcare providers reach the right patients with the right message, so they can grow their practices." **Patient acquisition for individual practices — not institutional commercial systems.**
- **Conversion mechanic.** "Book Now" / "Speak with a Health Marketing Expert."
- **Lead magnet.** Downloadable "how to gain more patients" guide; a marketing podcast (Sam Talks Marketing); a productised services shop.
- **Pricing transparency.** Partial — a "Shop" with productised service packages, more transparent than most.
- **Mobile / build.** Brizy/WordPress; heavyish. Editorial quality is uneven (visible typos: "MARKTING," "strengtffffhened"). A credibility leak GSLEY's anti-slop discipline avoids.
- **AI-search readiness.** Silent, though the page carries a decent FAQ block that is incidentally citable.
- **Named methodology.** None.
- **GSLEY's gap to exploit.** SA Media owns the *"healthcare marketing Lagos"* search term but plays at the SME/practitioner altitude — patient acquisition for clinics. GSLEY's buyer is the hospital board, the foundation director, the parastatal lead. The risk is purely search-term collision, not positioning collision: GSLEY's content must make its altitude explicit so an institutional buyer doesn't mistake the two.

### 2.4 Brandtelling — US named-methodology comparator
*brandtelling.com · live fetch · the closest register match to GSLEY*

- **Positioning sentence.** "Brand Architecture for Growth-Stage Firms." Counter-positioned sharply: "Brandtelling isn't a rebrand shop. We're a structural brand architecture firm."
- **Hero.** "Replace Narrative Drift with a Story Operating System™." Pain-led bullets directly beneath ("Every sales call uses a different story — and prospects notice").
- **Conversion mechanic.** "Schedule a Brand Architecture Review"; a lower-commitment "Book a Brand Narrative Audit" as the entry rung.
- **Lead magnet.** A published book (*The Art of Brandtelling*) with a free chapter; a podcast. Authority-led.
- **Pricing transparency.** Opaque, but the productised ladder is visible and named: Brand Narrative Audit → Story Operating System™ Installation → Brand Management Subscription. The services nav is literally titled **"The Ladder."**
- **Mobile / build.** Squarespace; clean, restrained, decent performance.
- **AI-search readiness.** Moderate — strong FAQ and structured copy, cited statistics with named sources (Made to Stick; Demand Metric/Lucidpress; Biznology; Demand Gen Report), but not explicitly LLM-targeted.
- **Named methodology.** **Strong — Story Operating System™, trademarked, threaded through every page.**
- **GSLEY's parallel.** This is the model GSLEY is already running, validated. The differences are GSLEY's advantages: Brandtelling is single-pillar (brand narrative only), single-discipline, US small-firm. GSLEY is two-pillar (Market + Digital), AI-native, and institutional. The lesson to copy: name the methodology, cite every statistic, make the value ladder the navigation, lead the founder as the authority.

## 4 · The one fix the teardown demands: name the methodology

Every strong comparator owns a trademarked or proper-noun methodology — Brandtelling's *Story Operating System™*, Kalungi's *GTM Operating System*, GROW's *AI-First GTM Engine*. GSLEY currently says *"documented archetypes — never opinions,"* which is a strong principle but not yet a branded asset an LLM or a buyer can latch onto.

The existing site already uses *Archetype* in its vocabulary, and the service-page template (brief §1.3.2) names a *"§ THE ARCHETYPE"* block. Formalise it. Two-level structure recommended:

- **Umbrella methodology (one proper noun).** Candidates, in the existing register:
  1. **The Archetype System™** — directly extends existing language; "documented archetypes" becomes the system.
  2. **The Compounding System™** — extends the existing "compounding, not campaigns" line; commercially evocative.
  3. **The Gap Engine™** — extends the thesis "we engineer the gap."

  *Recommendation: The Archetype System™.* It is already half-built in the current copy, it counter-positions cleanly against "opinions," and it gives every service page a named parent.

- **Per-service archetypes (one named archetype each).** Each service's "§ THE ARCHETYPE" block carries its own proper noun — e.g., the Demand Generation service runs *The Named-Account Demand Archetype*; AI Agent Deployment runs *The Workflow-First Deployment Archetype*. Drafted per-service at Turns 8–11.

This is a founder decision. Chukwudi picks the umbrella name; the team builds the per-service archetypes around it.
